Did you know?

The world's largest waterfall is underwater.

The Denmark Strait Cataract sits between Greenland and Iceland. Twice the height of Angel Falls, it carries 2,000 times more water than Niagara Falls, and is entirely invisible beneath the surface. Climate change is threatening to destroy it.
